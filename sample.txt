ERCOT Energy Report 2024

The WA Parish power plant located in South Texas has been upgraded
to 3,800 MW capacity. It primarily uses natural gas and supplies
power to the HB_SOUTH hub. The plant is owned by NRG Energy.

HB_NORTH hub receives power from multiple sources including the
Comanche Peak nuclear facility and several wind farms in West Texas.
The total capacity serving HB_NORTH exceeds 5,000 MW.

Renewable energy now accounts for 30% of ERCOT's total generation,
with wind and solar being the primary sources.
